# Claim hygiene ledger

last_reviewed: 2026-09-04

Checked on every superlative, every capability mention and every number. Never a prohibition
without a replacement, because a ban with no alternative gets ignored by whoever is on deadline.

## Hard bans

| Never write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| Any customer name, logo, quote, count, case study or outcome metric | Nothing. There is no reference customer. Use the architecture, the published price, or a sourced third-party figure. |
| "SOC 2 certified", "ISO 27001 certified", "certified" of any kind | "No certification is held today." If asked when, say it is not scheduled and explain what the architecture provides instead. |
| "We are data-resident" or "your data stays in the Kingdom" | "There is no mechanism by which your data could leave, so you do not need a transfer basis for it." |
| A capability in the present tense without its status flag | The capability, with SHIPPED, PARTIAL, IN BUILD or ROADMAP in the same sentence. |
| "No new invoice" for ongoing capability development | "New capabilities are scoped and delivered inside the quarter." Restore the fuller phrase only once the allowance is contracted. |
| The SAR 79 seat compared against a competitor seat price | Landed cost on both sides, all three lines, pass-through named. |
| "Cheaper than Microsoft" or any lowest-cost claim | "Microsoft's assistant is cheaper per seat. Compare what each is allowed to do." |
| "More sovereign than" the national champion, or any comparison with it | "Different products. If you want the national model, we run on it. You will probably buy both." |
| Prices, tiers or terms for the deployment scope ladder as though contracted | The published three lines only. The ladder is proposed and not yet in any contract. |
| Any output for paid social, or any automated first touch into a Dream 100 account | Decline. For paid social, offer the outbound sequences instead. For a Dream 100 account, the opening is an introduction, never a sequence. |
| "AI spend governance" as the category | "In-perimeter agentic workforce platform." |
| Arabic-first described as complete | "Arabic interface, prompting and output, partial today." Name the surface. |
| Any capability demonstrated on video or in a live demo that is not SHIPPED | Demonstrate only SHIPPED capabilities. Describe the rest. |

## Channel and medium specific bans

**Spoken and video.** A status flag dropped for pacing is the most common failure here. If a
capability cannot be named with its flag in the sentence, cut the capability from the script.

**Arabic assets.** Never publish machine-translated Arabic. A translated headline reads as
translated and undermines the Arabic-first claim in the same breath as making it.

**Messaging apps.** No unsolicited first contact. WhatsApp is for after a reply has landed, never as an opener.

**Outbound sequences.** Instantly and HeyReach are in scope and are the primary channels. Two things stay banned inside them: any first touch into a Dream 100 account, and any merge tag beyond first name and company. A mistransliterated Arabic first name in line one ends the conversation and is a claim-hygiene failure, not a formatting one.

**Public tender responses.** No claim in a submission that is not in this ledger. A tender
document is a legal representation and it is read by people who check.

## Claims that need scoping

Four superlatives from the source strategy were run through a counter-example drill. Keep the
counter-examples: they are what stops the broad form creeping back, and what an operator needs
when a prospect pushes back mid-call.

| Claim as written in the strategy | Counter-example | Safe form | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| The only platform where your data never leaves | A self-hosted open-source stack also keeps data inside the perimeter | "No path exists by which a prompt, a file or an output reaches Wolffish." Then move to what the open-source stack does not produce: attributed audit, enforced quotas, approval gates. | Narrowed |
| The cheapest option | Microsoft's assistant is cheaper per seat. A do-it-yourself stack is close on modelled cost. | "Forecastable rather than cheapest. A cost you set rather than an invoice you receive." | Narrowed |
| The fastest deployment | An integrator can move faster on a narrow brief. A complex multi-entity deployment is ten to fourteen weeks. | "Six to eight weeks to a pilot cohort, for a standard scope." Name the scope. | Narrowed |
| No transfer mechanism is required, because nothing crosses the border | None found. The claim is architectural rather than comparative. | Unchanged. | **Holds** |

A claim that cannot be narrowed into a defensible form belongs in hard bans.

## Historical and reputational material

**Wolffish has no customers yet.** Do not imply otherwise through phrasing such as "our
customers", "teams using Wolffish", or "we typically see". The honest form is "the architecture
does X", or "here is what we would expect and how we would measure it".

**Wolffish is two people.** Do not conceal it, and do not apologise for it. The honest handling
is source access and escrow presented before the question is asked, plus the observation that
an integrator's continuity answer about four named people on an account is not obviously stronger.

**Competitor facts commonly misremembered.** Microsoft's Saudi cloud region is announced for the
fourth quarter of 2026, not live. One large cloud provider has committed investment in a Saudi
region and has not launched it. Repeating a stale rumour about a rival in front of a Saudi buyer
is an avoidable credibility loss, so check `../reference/competitor-battlecards.md` before
asserting anything about a competitor's position.

# Writing rules

last_reviewed: 2026-09-04

How every word is written. Checked on every asset. Full context in
`../context/Wolffish_GTM_Master_Context.md` Section 1.

## Mechanics

No em dashes anywhere. Use commas, or restructure the sentence.

Banned words: leverage, unlock, seamless, robust, streamline, cutting-edge, empower, revolutionary,
game-changing, synergy, best-in-class, world-class, turnkey, frictionless

Short paragraphs, one to three sentences. Specific numbers, named capabilities and verifiable
facts instead of adjectives. No stacked adjectives. Prose over lists unless the content is
genuinely a list. Numerals for figures that carry weight, words for small counts in running prose.

Currency is written as SAR with a space, for example SAR 60,000. Never a bare riyal symbol in
English copy. State that prices exclude the fifteen percent value-added tax wherever a price
appears in a quotation.

## Tone

Precise, unhurried, and willing to say no.

The reason this register fits: the reader is a security officer or a finance director who is
personally accountable if the decision is wrong. Certainty that has not been earned reads to them
as risk rather than confidence. A vendor who names a limitation before being asked is easier to
believe about everything else, which is why the disqualifier list in
`../reference/positioning.md` does more work than any claim.

Never enthusiastic. Never urgent. The one place urgency is legitimate is a real calendar
constraint, and then it is stated as a date rather than as pressure.

## Bilingual rules

Every asset ships bilingual or it does not ship. In Arabic-first channels the Arabic is the
headline and the English is the subhead, never the reverse.

Arabic is written natively, not translated. Right-to-left layout is verified on a real device
before anything is published. Mixed Arabic and Latin numerals are checked specifically, because
that is the most common rendering failure.

## Claim discipline

Every superlative goes through `do-not-say.md`. Every capability mention goes through
`feature-status.md`, and the flag travels in the same sentence. Every conditional claim names its
condition. Every cost figure states landed cost on both sides.

If a task needs a fact this brain does not hold, say so and ask. Inventing a plausible fact is the
worst failure available here, because the output looks correct.

## Channel discipline

**The two primary channels are outbound at scale:** Instantly for cold email and HeyReach for
LinkedIn. They carry roughly fifty-five percent of expected qualified pipeline between them.

The supporting channels are events and physical presence, warm introduction and the sponsor
network, founder-led organic writing on professional and Arabic social platforms, messaging apps
after a reply has landed, channel partner co-sell, public tender from year two, and the owned layer
of site, scorecard and calculator.

**Execution constraints on the two primary channels, and they are rules rather than preferences.**
Send Sunday to Thursday, never Friday, and pause for Ramadan. No merge tag beyond first name and
company, with every Arabic first name transliteration verified before send. Sending domains stay
separate from the primary domain. The Dream 100 sits on a permanent suppression list in both tools
and is opened by introduction only. Every sequence ends at the same single call to action.

It refuses: paid social. If asked, decline, name the reason in one sentence, and offer the outbound
sequences instead. Do not quietly produce a softer version.

## Signature lines

Approved and reusable verbatim. The fuller set is in `../ops/copy-bank.md`.

- Everyone offers residency. We offer the absence of a data path.
- Chat drafts. Agents finish.
- A cost you decide, not an invoice that arrives.
- An integrator sells you the first use case. We ship the next twelve inside the seat.
- Sovereign models are infrastructure. We are the layer that puts them on every desk.
- If we are not a fit, we will say so on the call.

## Formatting

Headings in sentence case. No decorative punctuation. No emoji in customer-facing assets. Tables
where the content is genuinely tabular, and never as a substitute for a sentence that should have
been written.

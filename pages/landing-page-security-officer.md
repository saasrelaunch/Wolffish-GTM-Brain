# What Your AI Programme Desperately Needs

## Residency You Can Point At, Not Defend

Every vendor in this market offers the same thing: a contractual commitment about how they will handle your data after they receive it. That commitment is a document, and you are the one who defends it to the regulator. The personal data law reaches SAR 5 million per breach and doubles on repeat, the authority issued forty-eight enforcement decisions in the year to early 2026, and no adequacy list has been published. What you need is not a better promise. You need an architecture where the question does not arise, because there is no mechanism by which your data could leave.

## A Cost You Write Instead Of Receive

On a metered plan, the more the agents work the more the company pays. Finance responds the only way it can, by rationing exactly the behaviour that creates the value, and your AI programme quietly becomes a budget that people are told not to spend. Forecastability is not a nice-to-have here. It is what lets you say yes to a rollout in September and still know the number in March.

## Evidence That Survives The Audit Committee

Classification exists in almost every Saudi enterprise. What almost none of them have is a record showing which employee asked an AI system to do what, to which data, under whose approval, and where that record is stored. Until that record exists in your own environment, every AI decision in the building is unevidenced, and the first person to discover that will be someone from outside the company.

# But You're Still:

## Watching Classification Stop At The AI Decision

The register says which datasets are restricted. It does not reach the moment an employee pastes a restricted record into a prompt, because the prompt is not a system in your inventory and the paste is not an event anything logs. Classification on paper that does not reach the decision is the single most common gap in Saudi enterprise, and it is invisible until someone asks you to evidence it.

## Watching Your Identity Estate Stop At The Vendor's Front Door

You can offboard a leaver from every system you own in under an hour. You cannot tell whether their prompts from last quarter still sit in a vendor's inference logs, because that is not your environment and the answer is not yours to produce. Your identity controls end precisely where your exposure begins.

## Holding A Contractual Promise Where You Needed A Mechanism

The security review ended in a data processing agreement. A data processing agreement is not a transfer basis. When your data protection officer is asked which mechanism permits the transfer, the contract is the wrong document, and under the cloud regulations public-sector data may not leave the Kingdom for any purpose, permanently or temporarily, including caching. Caching alone breaches it. Nobody reads the caching clause until it matters.

Eighty-one percent of Saudi enterprises already deploy AI, and the ones who have not sanctioned it have it anyway: personal accounts, company data in the prompt, no transfer basis on file and no record you could produce. Not banning it was the right call. It also means the exposure is already live and currently unmeasured.

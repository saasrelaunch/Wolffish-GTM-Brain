# Template, warm introduction

Audience: the sponsor who will forward, not the target. Channel: warm introduction, the single
largest human channel. Sequence and sponsor mechanics: `../reference/relationship-engine.md`.

## The two parts
An introduction is two documents. The ask to the sponsor, which is private, and the forwardable
paragraph, which the sponsor pastes without editing. Most introductions fail because only the
first was written.

## Part one, the ask to the sponsor
- **The named target.** One person, one company. Never a list, because a list transfers the work
  of choosing to the person doing you a favour.
- **Why this person specifically.** One sentence naming the accountability the target holds.
- **What you are asking for.** A thirty-minute conversation, not a meeting about a purchase.
- **The out.** An explicit sentence giving the sponsor permission to decline without explanation.
  This is what makes the second ask possible.

## Part two, the forwardable paragraph
- Under eighty words.
- No attachment. No tracked link. Both convert a personal favour into a marketing action and both
  are visible to the recipient.
- Names the regulatory or cost problem in the target's own terms, not the product.
- One sentence on Wolffish, factual, with any capability status flag attached.
- Ends with the specific and small ask.

## Fields to fill
- Sponsor: [name, relationship, what they get from making this work]
- Target: [name, role, entity, the accountability they hold]
- The problem in their terms: [one clause]
- The ask: [thirty-minute conversation, at their office, in the next three weeks]

## Timing
Do not send an introduction request in the two weeks before a major national holiday, during the
first week of Ramadan, or in the final week of a fiscal quarter for a listed entity. See the
calendar section of `../reference/customer-journey.md`.

Guardrail: no proof point outside the closed set in `../ops/copy-bank.md`, no capability without
its flag from `../rules/feature-status.md`, and run `../ops/QA-checklist.md` on the forwardable
paragraph before it leaves your hands, because you will never see it again after it does.

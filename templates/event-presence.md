# Template, event presence

Audience: pick one per artefact. Channel: events and physical presence, the largest single share
of expected pipeline. Two fixed dates carry the year, so the deadlines are external and immovable.

## The three artefacts an event needs
An event is not one asset. It is a speaking abstract submitted months early, a stand conversation
that lasts ninety seconds, and a follow-up sent inside the same week. Skipping the third wastes
the first two.

## One, the speaking abstract
- **Title, bilingual.** Arabic first on any Arabic-first programme.
- **The problem in one sentence,** stated as a regulatory or accountability problem, not a product
  category.
- **Three things the audience will be able to do afterwards.** Concrete, testable.
- **Why this speaker.** Named engineering experience, not adjectives.
- No product pitch anywhere in the abstract. Programme committees reject vendor talks and the
  audience you want can tell the difference from the title alone.

## Two, the stand conversation
- **Opening question,** not an opening statement. The question qualifies in one exchange, for
  example which regulator sets their data rules.
- **The ninety-second answer** if they qualify: what the product is, where the work happens, what
  it costs, in that order.
- **The disqualifier said out loud.** Naming who this is not for is the fastest trust move
  available at a stand and it saves both parties an evaluation.
- **The single next step.** A security call, scheduled on the spot, at their office.

## Three, the same-week follow-up
- Sent within five working days or it is not sent.
- Refers to something specific that was said, which is the only proof the conversation happened.
- Carries the one-pager for the audience that person belongs to, and nothing else.

# Template, founder post, bilingual

Audience: pick one, security officer or finance director. Channel: founder organic writing.
Method, archetypes and edit discipline: `../reference/relationship-engine.md`.

## Pick the archetype first
One of four, rotated: instrument explainer, cost-shock note, deployment note, refusal note.
Do not mix two archetypes in one post. Mixing produces a post that teaches nothing and sells
nothing.

## Structure
- **Arabic headline.** One line, written natively. This is the headline, not a translation of the
  English one.
- **English subhead.** One line, under the Arabic.
- **The specific.** Name the instrument, the article, the number or the date. A post that opens
  with a generality is scrolled past by the only twenty people it needed to reach.
- **What it means for an artificial intelligence decision.** Two to four sentences, in the reader's
  own accountability terms.
- **What to ask a vendor.** Two or three questions the reader can put to any vendor including
  Wolffish. This is the part that gets forwarded.
- **Close.** No product mention in the instrument explainer archetype. In the other three, at most
  one sentence, and it names a limitation as readily as a capability.

## Fields to fill
- Archetype: [instrument explainer / cost-shock note / deployment note / refusal note]
- Audience: [security officer / finance director]
- The instrument or number: [cite it exactly, with its issuing body]
- The question set: [two or three]

## Length and cadence
Between 120 and 250 words. One post a week. The series compounds and cannot be shortcut, which is
why the cadence matters more than any single post.

## What this post may never do
No customer name, quote, metric or count. There is no reference customer, so any such detail is
fabrication. See `../rules/do-not-say.md`.

No positioning against the national champion. Neutralise in one sentence if the subject arises,
then move on.

No call to book a call at the end of an instrument explainer. The archetype earns its credibility
by not selling, and one sale attempt spends the credibility of the whole series.

Guardrail: check every claim against `../rules/do-not-say.md`, every capability against
`../rules/feature-status.md`, and run `../ops/QA-checklist.md` before posting.

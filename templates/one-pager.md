# Template, bilingual one-page leave-behind

Audience: one, never two. There is a security officer version and a finance director version, and
they are different documents. Channel: events and warm introductions.

## Layout
Arabic on the left column or the first face, English on the second. The Arabic is written
natively. A translated Arabic column is visible as such to every reader who matters, and it costs
more credibility than an English-only page would.

One page. If it does not fit, the audience is not narrow enough yet.

## Structure
- **Header.** What Wolffish Cloud is, one line, from `../reference/positioning.md`.
- **The problem in their terms.** Two sentences. For the security officer this is exposure and
  accountability. For the finance director it is unbounded and unattributable cost.
- **Three pillars.** One line each, taken from that audience's value proposition file,
  `../reference/value-proposition-icp1.md` or `../reference/value-proposition-icp2.md`.
- **Where the work happens.** One line. In-perimeter, zero-retention endpoint, no vendor-side data
  path.
- **What is live today.** Plain statement, with anything unshipped labelled in the same sentence.
  This block is what a competent evaluator turns to first.
- **The three price lines.** Platform, deployment, pass-through, with the words landed cost, and
  the note that prices exclude the fifteen percent value-added tax.
- **One next step.** A security call with the security team in the room.

## Fields to fill
- Audience: [security officer / finance director]
- The three pillars for that audience: [from the value proposition file, verbatim]
- Live-today statement: [checked line by line against feature status]

## What this page may never carry
No customer logo, quote, count or case study, because there is no reference customer.
No unscoped superlative. Every only, first, best and never goes through `../rules/do-not-say.md`.
No seat price standing alone. A seat price without the other two lines understates landed cost by
roughly a third and a finance director will find it.

Guardrail: one audience, every capability flagged from `../rules/feature-status.md`, every claim
checked against `../rules/do-not-say.md`, and `../ops/QA-checklist.md` run before printing.
Printing is the point at which a mistake becomes expensive.

# Template, owned site page

Audience: one per page. Channel: owned, Arabic first. The site underpins every other channel
because it is what a sponsor checks before agreeing to make an introduction, and what a security
officer reads before the call.

## Arabic first, natively
Arabic is the primary language of the page and English is the secondary. Not a language toggle
bolted onto an English site. The visitor who matters can tell within one paragraph whether the
Arabic was written or translated, and that judgment transfers to everything else on the page.

## Structure
- **Hero.** What Wolffish Cloud is, where the work happens, who it is for. Three facts, no
  adjectives.
- **The problem,** in the language of the audience this page serves.
- **The three pillars** for that audience, from their value proposition file.
- **Architecture.** A diagram and a paragraph. In-perimeter execution, zero-retention endpoint, no
  vendor-side data path.
- **Status.** What is live today, what is in build, each flagged. Publishing this is a positioning
  move in a market where competitors publish nothing.
- **The three price lines, published.** Platform, deployment, pass-through, with landed cost named
  and the value-added tax note. Publishing price against a market of quote-only competitors is
  itself the differentiator.
- **Who this is not for.** The disqualifier list, published. It converts better than any claim on
  the page above it.
- **One next step.** The security call, or the scorecard.

## Fields to fill
- Audience this page serves: [security officer / finance director / partner]
- Pillars: [from the matching value proposition file]
- Next step: [one, not two]

## Standing constraints
No customer logo, testimonial, count or case study anywhere on the site. There is no reference
customer and a placeholder logo wall is a claim.

No form that promises a response time the founder cannot meet at a delivery ceiling of four to six
deployments a year.

Guardrail: one audience per page, every capability flagged from `../rules/feature-status.md`,
every claim through `../rules/do-not-say.md`, and `../ops/QA-checklist.md` run before publishing.
A published page is a claim that stays published.

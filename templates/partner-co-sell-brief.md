# Template, partner co-sell brief

Audience: a system integrator, a cloud partner or a managed security provider, not an end
customer. Channel: partner co-sell, about ten percent of year-one pipeline and more later. It is
what converts the second ICP from unreachable to reachable.

## Write this only when a specific partner is in view
Building partner enablement speculatively is a common and expensive mistake. If you cannot name
the partner, the account they hold and the person who would carry this, stop.

## Structure
- **What the partner gets.** Revenue mechanics first, in their model, not ours. A partner reads
  the margin line before anything else and will not read past a page that opens with our product.
- **Where we fit in their delivery.** The named gap in their current stack, stated without
  disparaging what they already sell.
- **What we do not do.** The boundary of the deployment scope ladder, so their delivery team can
  price their own side.
- **What we need from them.** The introduction, the account, the joint security call. Be specific,
  because a vague partnership produces vague activity.
- **Capacity, stated honestly.** Delivery capacity is four to six deployments in twelve months. A
  partner who is told this plans around it. A partner who discovers it later stops selling.
- **The first joint motion.** One named account, one date.

## Fields to fill
- Partner and the person: [organisation, name, role]
- The account in view: [entity]
- Their gap we fill: [one clause]
- The revenue mechanic proposed: [reseller margin, referral fee, or joint delivery split]

## Local content
Saudi local content and the preference regime affect how a partner scores a joint bid. Read the
local content section of `../reference/saudi-regulatory-file.md` before proposing any structure,
because the structure changes the partner's own score and that is often the deciding factor.

Guardrail: no capability offered to a partner that is not flagged in `../rules/feature-status.md`,
no capacity implied beyond the stated ceiling, no pricing outside the published three lines. Run
`../ops/QA-checklist.md`, and log what the partner asked for into `../ops/signal-log.md`.

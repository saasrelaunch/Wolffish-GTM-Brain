# Snapshot, 2026-09-04, baseline

The state of the go-to-market on the day the brain was built. Everything here is a starting
position rather than a measurement, because nothing has been sold yet. That is the point of a
baseline: the next snapshot is read against it, and the difference is the evidence.

Delivery ceiling for the coming twelve months is four to six deployments. The funnel is measured
against that ceiling, not against a conversion rate.

## 2. Channel

Nothing has run. Expected shares are the plan, not a result: events about thirty-five percent,
warm introduction about thirty, founder writing about fifteen, messaging about ten, partner
co-sell about ten in year one, tender from year two, owned assets underpinning all of them.

The two fixed dates that shape the year are the September and December events. About twenty-five of
forty-eight weeks are sellable once holidays, Ramadan and the summer are removed.

## 3. Segment

Two audiences, unvalidated in the field. ICP 1, Saudi-owned regulated enterprises of 200 to 600
knowledge workers, sold founder-led. ICP 2, larger regulated institutions reached through partners.
Whether ICP 2 is reachable at all in year one is the first thing the next snapshot should answer.

## 4. Wins and losses

None. No deal has been won, lost or abandoned. The first loss reason will be more informative than
the first win, and it goes into the signal log verbatim.

## 5. Competitive

Microsoft's assistant is cheaper per seat and its Saudi region is announced for the fourth quarter
of 2026, not live. The national champion is treated as neutralised rather than competed with.
Integrators are the partner channel and the substitute at the same time. No competitive move has
been observed in the field, only in public material.

## 6. Product status

Read against `../rules/feature-status.md` on this date. The agentic harness and the platform
control plane are almost entirely SHIPPED. Arabic-first coverage is PARTIAL across four surfaces.
Source access and escrow is PARTIAL, deed drafted and no agent appointed. The deployment scope
ladder and the capability allowance are IN BUILD and, more importantly, not contracted. The
Compliance Evidence Pack is IN BUILD and is the largest unbuilt asset in the plan.

No certification is held.

## 7. Economics

Published and fixed: SAR 60,000 deployment, SAR 79 per seat per month, SAR 45 to 55 per seat per
month of pass-through billed by the customer. Landed cost SAR 124 to 184 per employee per month.
Breakeven at 300 seats is a modelled productivity gain of 0.77 percent. All modelled, none observed.

## What this changes

Nothing, because there is nothing yet to change. This snapshot exists to be the thing the next one
is measured against.

Two numbers to watch in the next capture, because they are the ones that would force a strategy
change rather than a copy change:

**The security call decline rate.** Declining above fifteen percent of security calls is a target
rather than an accident. A rate near zero means the disqualifier list is not being used, which
means the pipeline is filling with accounts that cannot buy.

**Weeks of founder time per deployment.** If the first deployment consumes materially more than the
six to eight week standard scope, the delivery ceiling is lower than four to six, and the named
account list has to shrink before anything else is decided.

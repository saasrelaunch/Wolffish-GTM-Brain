### Wolffish Cloud
**AI agents that run on the employee's machine, with inference on your own account.**

**The problem.** Your team is being asked to approve AI assistants, and the rules make you evidence
what happens to organisational data in the hands of a third party executing against it. Most
vendors offer a contractual commitment. You are the one who defends it to the regulator.

**Three pillars**
1. Nothing of yours leaves the building. Agents execute on the employee's own machine, inference
   goes to a zero-retention endpoint on your account, and no path exists by which a prompt, a file
   or an output reaches Wolffish.
2. The action-level audit log lives in your own store, attributed to an employee and a timestamp,
   with approval gates on sensitive operations and a kill switch per user and globally.
3. Because nothing crosses the border, no transfer mechanism is required and the evidence pack is
   shorter than the alternative.

**Where the work happens.** In-perimeter execution, zero-retention endpoint, no vendor-side data
path. Everyone else offers residency. This is the absence of a data path, which is a different
claim.

**What is live today.** Persistent per-employee agents, file access and shell execution scoped per
role, approval gates, hard token quota, action-level audit log, kill switch, single sign-on with
directory sync and offboarding, and Saudi commercial registration with riyal contracting, all
SHIPPED. Support for a sovereign in-Kingdom inference endpoint, SHIPPED as a model option. Arabic
interface, prompting, output and document handling, PARTIAL, and we will show you exactly which
surfaces. Source access and escrow, PARTIAL, the deed is drafted and no agent is appointed. The
Compliance Evidence Pack, IN BUILD, six weeks.

**What it costs.** SAR 60,000 deployment once, SAR 79 per seat per month to Wolffish, and SAR 45 to
55 per seat per month billed by your own providers at zero Wolffish margin. Landed cost SAR 124 to

**Who this is not for.** Organisations without a directory to sync, under 200 knowledge workers, or
looking for a chatbot rather than agents that execute. We will say so on the first call.

## Why this works

**One audience.** Every line answers an accountability question. The finance director version of
this page leads with the bill and would read as evasive to this reader, which is why they are two
documents.

**The status block is specific and includes what is not finished.** A page that flags PARTIAL and
IN BUILD is the page a competent evaluator believes on everything else. This block is what they
turn to first.

**The distinction is stated as a distinction.** Naming residency as the thing everyone offers, and
the absence of a data path as the different claim, does the positioning work in two sentences
without a superlative.

**The disqualifier list is printed on the page.** It converts better than any claim above it,
because a vendor who publishes who they are wrong for is easier to believe about who they are right
for.

**All three price lines appear on a security officer's page.** The finance director will read this
page eventually, without you, and a seat price alone would be the thing they remember.

**No customer evidence anywhere.** No logo, quote, count or case study, because there is no
reference customer. The Arabic was written natively rather than translated.

# Example, the forwardable paragraph

Audience: written for the sponsor to paste, read by the target. Channel: warm introduction.
Approved 2026-09-04.

---

## The asset, the private ask to the sponsor

Ahmed, a favour, and please say no if it is awkward.

I would like thirty minutes with the chief information security officer at [entity], at their
office, in the next three weeks. Not a pitch, and not about a purchase. Their team is being asked
to approve AI assistants, and the cloud rules make that harder than most vendors admit. I would
like to hear how they are handling it, and I can show what we built.

If you would rather not, that is genuinely fine and it will not come up again.

If you are willing, the paragraph below is written for you to paste, unedited, and it carries no
attachment and no tracked link.

---

## The asset, the forwardable paragraph

Introducing you to the founder of Wolffish Cloud, a Saudi-registered company building AI agents
that run on the employee's own machine, with inference on the customer's own account, so employee
data does not leave the organisation's perimeter. Given the cloud rules on data leaving the
Kingdom, I thought the architecture was worth thirty minutes of your team's time. He is not
selling anything today.

*Seventy-one words.*

---

## Why this works

**It is two documents, and most introductions only write the second.** The private ask is what
gets the sponsor to act. The paragraph is what survives the forward. Writing one without the other
is why introduction requests go unanswered.

**The out is explicit.** "Please say no if it is awkward" and the closing sentence cost nothing and
are what make a second ask possible six months later. In a market where the same twenty accounts
recur, the relationship outlives the deal.

**One named target.** Not a list. A list transfers the work of choosing to the person doing you a
favour, which is the fastest way to receive nothing.

**No attachment and no tracked link.** Both are visible to the recipient and both convert a
personal favour into a marketing action, which is exactly what the sponsor was avoiding by making
an introduction.

**The paragraph is architectural, not promotional.** It names where the work happens and why that
matters under a rule the reader already knows about. The only claim in it, that data does not leave
the perimeter, is the claim the brain permits and can evidence.

**It closes the sales door on purpose.** "He is not selling anything today" is what makes the
thirty minutes cheap to grant. It also has to be true on the call, which is why the security call
agenda opens with architecture and not with price.

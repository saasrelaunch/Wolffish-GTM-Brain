# Example, messaging app follow-up

Audience: one named person, already introduced. Channel: messaging apps, never a first touch.
Approved 2026-09-04.

---

## The asset

Good morning Dr Faisal. Ahmed introduced us at the security forum last Tuesday.

The cloud regulator published its updated registration categories this week. The relevant part for
your team is that the categories are set against customer data classification, not against vendor
size, which changes which providers can hold level three data. I have put the two paragraphs that
matter in a note if it is useful.

No rush on a reply.

*Fifty-eight words.*

---

## The asset, the second message, sent a week later after no reply

Understood that this is a busy period. I will leave it here. If the classification question comes
back around, I am easy to find.

*Twenty-six words.*

---

## Why this works

**The precondition was met.** An introduction landed first. A message to a Saudi executive who has
not been introduced is not a channel, it is an intrusion, and it costs the sponsor as well as the
sender.

**One line of context, and it names the sponsor.** The reader places the sender without scrolling
and the sponsor's name carries the credibility, not the company's.

**One substantive thing they would want even if they never buy.** A regulatory update, specific
enough to be useful, with the implication stated. This is the whole message. The offer of a note is
incidental to it.

**No attachment on the first message.** A document arrives after the reply, not before it. Sending
one unrequested makes the message a marketing action and it will be read as one.

**One ask, softly, and an explicit release of pressure.** "No rush on a reply" costs nothing and is
the difference between a message that gets a late reply and one that gets none.

**The second message closes rather than chases.** There is no third. In a market of twenty named
accounts, the relationship outlives this deal, and the follow-up that damages it also damages the
sponsor who made the introduction.

**Nothing here carries a price, a term or a security answer.** Those go into a document that can be
filed and audited. A security officer who receives a compliance answer over a messaging app learns
something about how this vendor handles evidence.

# Example, bilingual founder post, instrument explainer

Archetype: instrument explainer. Audience: security officer. Channel: founder organic writing.
Approved 2026-09-04.

---

## The asset

**قاعدة واحدة في تنظيمات الحوسبة السحابية تُخرج أغلب مساعدات الذكاء الاصطناعي من نطاق العمل الحكومي**

*One clause in the cloud computing regulations rules most AI assistants out of public sector work.*

تنص التنظيمات على أن بيانات الجهات الحكومية لا يجوز نقلها خارج المملكة لأي غرض، بشكل دائم أو مؤقت، بما في ذلك التخزين المؤقت والنسخ الاحتياطي.

الإشكال في الجملة الأخيرة. أي مساعد يرسل نص الموظف إلى نموذج مستضاف خارج المملكة يُنشئ نسخة عابرة خارج الحدود، سواء احتفظ بها أحد أم لا. والتعهد التعاقدي بعدم الاحتفاظ لا يعالج ذلك، لأن الحكم يتعلق بالنقل لا بالحفظ.

ثلاثة أسئلة تصلح لأي مورد، ونحن منهم:

١. أين يجري الاستدلال فعلياً، وعلى حساب من؟
٢. ما الذي يغادر جهاز الموظف، وإلى أين بالضبط؟
٣. أرِني السجل الذي يثبت الإجابتين، لا الوثيقة التي تعد بهما.

---

The cloud computing regulations state that government entity data may not be transferred outside
the Kingdom for any purpose, permanently or temporarily, including caching and redundancy.

The difficulty sits in that last clause. Any assistant that sends employee text to a model hosted
abroad creates a transient copy across the border, whether or not anyone keeps it. A contractual
no-retention commitment does not answer this, because the rule attaches to the transfer and not to
the storage.

Three questions that work on any vendor, ourselves included:

1. Where does inference actually run, and on whose account?
2. What leaves the employee's machine, and where exactly does it go?
3. Show me the record that proves the first two answers, not the document that promises them.

---

## Why this works

**It teaches one instrument and sells nothing.** There is no product mention, no call to action and
no company name in the body. That is the archetype, and it is what makes a two-person company
credible to a security officer who has never heard of it. The series compounds precisely because
each post is free.

**The Arabic is the headline and it was written, not translated.** A reader who matters can tell
inside one paragraph, and that judgment transfers to every other claim the company makes.

**The specific arrives in the first line.** The clause, not a generality about artificial
intelligence risk. A post that opens with a generality is scrolled past by the twenty people it
needed to reach.

**The three questions include us.** Inviting the reader to put the same question to Wolffish is
the whole credibility mechanism. It also happens to be the question set on which Wolffish scores
well, which is why it can afford to be neutral.

**It names the mechanism, not the reassurance.** The distinction between a rule that attaches to
transfer and one that attaches to storage is the argument. Everything else on the page is
scaffolding for it.

**What it does not do.** No superlative. No claim that Wolffish is the only vendor that meets this,
which would fail the claim ledger. No mention of the national champion. No customer evidence,
because none exists yet.

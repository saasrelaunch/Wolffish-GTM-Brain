## ⚠️ Standing caveats for this dashboard

**C1 — Not SaaS.** The three pillars map deliberately onto three of the four layers. VP1 is the
platform and Saudi layers (B and D). VP2 is the platform layer plus the pass-through economics.
VP3 is the harness and service layers (A and C). **No pillar is built on the harness layer alone**,
because that layer is commoditising quarterly and copy written on it has a twelve-month shelf
life.

**C2 — No self-serve.** Every CTA below is a conversation, a diagnostic or a document. **None is
"start free trial" and none can be.** Copy that implies self-serve will convert an enterprise
buyer into a disappointed one at the first click.

**C3 — Landed cost, both sides.** RTB 2.3 and every copy drill in VP2 state landed cost including
the customer's own inference and infrastructure. **Any shortened version of this copy that quotes
SAR 79 against a competitor's seat price is prohibited.** It is the single fastest way to lose a
CFO.

**C4 — Saudi only.** Every headline carries an Arabic version, and the Arabic is the primary in
Arabic-first channels rather than a translation appended to an English original. Three RTBs
(1.3, 2.2 and 3.1) reference Saudi regulation directly and do not travel.

**C5 — Offer metrics unreconciled.** RTB 3.3 promises new capabilities "inside the quarter,
without a new invoice." **This copy must not ship until the capability allowance in
`00-OFFER-METRICS-AUDIT.md` Finding 1 is agreed and contractually bounded.** As written it is the
most attractive sentence in the suite and the least defensible.

## Copy usage rules for the Kingdom

1. **Never lead with VP2 to a CISO or VP1 to a CFO.** Right message, wrong chair, and it reads as
   not having understood who you are talking to.
2. **The Arabic is primary in Arabic-first channels.** A translated English headline reads as
   translated and undermines the Arabic-first claim in the same breath as making it.
3. **Never shorten a VP2 claim to SAR 79 against a competitor seat price.** See caveat C3.
4. **Never use VP3 RTB 3.3 in writing until the capability allowance exists.** See caveat C5.
5. **Do not use superlatives about sovereignty.** HUMAIN owns that vector. Every Wolffish claim
   about data is architectural and specific, never patriotic.
6. **Cite the source when quoting a number.** Saudi enterprise buyers check, and a checked number
   that holds is worth more than three unchecked ones.
7. **Every asset ships bilingual or it does not ship.** A one-page English-only leave-behind in a
   room where the CFO prefers Arabic is a self-inflicted disadvantage.

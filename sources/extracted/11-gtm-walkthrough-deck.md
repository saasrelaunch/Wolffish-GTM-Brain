# SLIDE 1, Wolffish Cloud GTM, Nine Dashboards for the Saudi Market

- This suite is the complete go to market for Wolffish Cloud in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, built as nine dashboards, a lead magnet specification and this walkthrough.
- Four standing caveats govern every dashboard. One, this is not SaaS, it is an agentic harness plus a platform plus a cloud service plus a Saudi regulatory layer, and features are scored per layer. Two, there is no self serve motion, no free tier and no trial, so every funnel stage is a committee stage. Three, only one of the three price lines is Wolffish revenue, so every comparison is stated as landed cost. Four, this is Saudi Arabia only and does not travel.
- A fifth caveat is a live risk, not a framing note. The Service and Offer Matrix contains two self rated blocks by two people describing two different companies, and four rating cells are challenged in the offer metrics audit.
- The category claim is an in perimeter agentic workforce platform, in Arabic منصة وكلاء ذكيين داخل نطاق منشأتك, chosen because no competitor can adopt the label without changing what it is.
- **So what:** the dashboards exist to answer one question, which twenty Saudi accounts should a two person company spend its four to six annual deployment slots on, and how does it reach them.

# SLIDE 2, Product Feature Matrix Dashboard, What We Lead With

- Thirty six features scored on demand and competitiveness, each tagged to one of four layers, harness, platform, cloud service and Saudi regulatory fit, because a commodity in one layer can be the reason a deal closes in another.
- Nineteen features land in the hero quadrant. The four strongest are zero vendor side data path, PDPL compliance by architecture, first class integration into bespoke and legacy systems, and Saudi entity with SAR contracting and ZATCA invoicing.
- The uncomfortable finding is that the harness layer, the agent itself, is where Wolffish is least defensible and is commoditising fastest, so positioning must not rest there.
- Validation caught three rating violations. The most important resolution split the residency claim in two, because everyone can promise data residency and only an architecture with no vendor data path can remove the need for a transfer mechanism.
- The demand driver is the Agent Exposure and Spend Audit, a one week founder led read producing a residency exposure map and a twelve month cost forecast, both of which are structurally unfixable inside a vendor cloud product.
- **So what:** the audit is the reason a CISO and a CFO sit in the same room, and it produces the business case for the deployment without anything having to be gathered twice.

# SLIDE 3, Competitive Market Analysis Dashboard, Who Is Actually In The Room

- Seven competitors, chosen for who appears on a Saudi shortlist rather than who ranks in a global category. Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, Claude Enterprise with Claude Code, HUMAIN, the Saudi systems integrator custom build, Cursor with GitHub Copilot, and the do it yourself open source stack.
- Microsoft is the hardest competitor in the Kingdom, not OpenAI, because it has a Saudi entity, existing agreements almost everywhere, a Saudi cloud region landing in Q4 2026 and an ALLaM collaboration with HUMAIN.
- The nearest competitor on the two vectors Wolffish leads is the Saudi systems integrator, not any AI vendor, and that is the most consequential line in the dashboard.
- Anthropic is the most useful competitor Wolffish has, because the failure mode is public and CFO legible. Uber exhausted its 2026 AI budget by April and Microsoft cancelled most internal Claude Code licences.
- The do it yourself open source stack is the competitor that kills deals quietly, proposed by an ambitious head of digital in the meeting after Wolffish leaves, and it is defeated on governance artefacts rather than on price.
- **So what:** most Wolffish deals will be won or lost against a Saudi integrator and an internal build, so the sales motion is built for those two arguments rather than for a feature comparison against OpenAI.

# SLIDE 4, Positioning Dashboard, Five Vectors, Concede Three And Win Two

- Five positioning vectors are used rather than the standard four, because in the Kingdom sovereign legitimacy decides deals and omitting it produces a map that flatters Wolffish and then loses in the room.
- Competitors lead three. Anthropic leads frontier model capability at 9.6, Microsoft leads ecosystem breadth at 9.7, HUMAIN leads sovereign legitimacy at 9.8. All three are conceded openly, and the concession is what makes the rest credible.
- Wolffish leads two. Forecastable cost per employee at 9.4, and in perimeter execution with custom system reach at 9.6. Both descend from one architectural decision, so neither can be copied by a multi tenant cloud without it ceasing to be one.
- The hardest objection in the Kingdom is why not HUMAIN, and the answer is never to compete. Model portability makes ALLaM on an in Kingdom endpoint a supported configuration, which turns the objection into a sentence, if you want the national model we run on it.
- Six positioning statements are written, against the vendor clouds, against the residency answer every vendor gives, against the metered stack, against the integrator, against the sovereign champion and against the status quo.
- **So what:** the cost vector has a shelf life of a few years because vendors are shipping spend controls, so spend it now, and build the business on the perimeter and Saudi layers that will still be there when it closes.

# SLIDE 5, ICP Dashboard, Twenty Accounts, Not A Funnel

- ICP Level one is a Saudi owned regulated or IP sensitive enterprise with 200 to 600 knowledge workers, a named CISO who has already blocked a public AI tool, at least one internal system nobody will integrate with, and a single accessible decision authority who can sign without a tender.
- Eleven Saudi specific attributes are added to the standard profile, including regulator, NDMO data classification exposure, ownership concentration, procurement route, Nitaqat band, Arabic to English workforce mix and the relationship sponsor path.
- The two hundred seat floor is a hard qualification gate. Wolffish year one revenue is SAR 60,000 plus SAR 948 per seat, while deployment consumes six to eight weeks of principal time regardless of seat count, so below two hundred seats the engagement cannot repay the time at any point in the contract.
- The strongest single maturity signal is an organisation that has already run an AI pilot that stalled, because they have spent the enthusiasm and now want the governance parts.
- An anti ICP is included, with nine decline criteria applied on the first call, because a wrong customer does not cost a lost deal, it costs a quarter of the year's delivery capacity.
- **So what:** at a fixed cost in the only scarce currency, principal weeks, seat count is the only variable worth optimising, and two five hundred seat customers are worth more than five two hundred seat customers at less than half the delivery cost.

# SLIDE 6, Buying Committee Dashboard, Seven Personas And One Sequence

- The source three persona model, user, manager and decision maker, is structurally insufficient for Saudi enterprise, so four personas are added, the CISO gatekeeper, the CFO economic validator, the procurement and legal procedural gate, and the relationship sponsor who has no formal role and decides how everyone else first hears about you.
- The CISO holds an absolute veto and, in SAMA regulated entities, a formal one, since no cloud AI contract can be signed without prior regulator approval.
- The committee sequence is the highest leverage process decision in the suite. Sponsor introduction first, then the CISO with the decision maker present, then the CIO, then the CFO, then the pilot cohort, then the decision maker, and procurement last, handed a complete pack rather than a request.
- This inverts the standard Western sequence, which starts with the economic buyer and treats security as a late compliance hurdle, and which in the Kingdom produces a deal that dies in month four.
- The design partner profile asks for a named public reference, two peer introductions and a CISO to CISO call, and explicitly refuses to give free seats, an indefinite pilot or unbounded capability development.
- **So what:** winning the security call first converts the largest risk in the funnel into its most credible internal advocate, and in a market where CISOs all know each other, that advocate is also the next deal's introduction.

# SLIDE 7, Customer Journey Funnel Dashboard, Twenty Five Sellable Weeks

- Seven stages from problem awareness to renewal, with the security call moved from stage three to stage two, and with stage four defined as a paid pilot cohort inside the customer's perimeter rather than a trial, because there is no trial to define.
- Total cycle from first contact to signature is sixteen to thirty four weeks, plus six to eight weeks to go live.
- Calendar weeks are not selling weeks in the Kingdom. Ramadan runs approximately 8 February to 8 March 2027 with a statutory six hour working day, Eid follows, and Riyadh empties in July and August. That leaves roughly twenty five sellable weeks in twelve months, not forty eight.
- The live window is now. GAIN is 15 to 17 September 2026, Black Hat MEA is 1 to 3 December 2026, and the state budget statement lands in early December against a calendar fiscal year, so a deal that has not reached stage three by October is a next year deal.
- Four blockers recur, why not HUMAIN, two person supplier risk, the internal build proposal, and fiscal year timing. Each has a defined answer and a defined asset.
- **So what:** a plan that assumes twelve even months of Saudi pipeline is wrong by about forty percent of the year, and the deployment capacity ceiling and the calendar ceiling happen to land in the same place at four to six deployments.

# SLIDE 8, Lead Magnet, The Kingdom AI Exposure Scorecard

- Twelve questions, four minutes, Arabic first and right to left correct, producing a composite Kingdom AI Exposure Score out of one hundred plus two independent sub scores, one for residency exposure and one for cost trajectory.
- Twelve questions are used rather than the standard five to seven, because a six question composite would produce a number a CISO would not carry to an audit committee, and a number they will not carry internally has no value.
- Three result tiers. Governed, Exposed and Unmapped, with the strongest tier offering a free one week founder led audit rather than a product, because a high scorer offered a trial takes the trial and never speaks to anyone.
- The seven step follow up sequence respects the Sunday to Thursday working week, never sends on Friday or Saturday, shifts to evening hours during Ramadan, and never uses WhatsApp before an introduction.
- It converts because it does not argue for Wolffish. It measures two things, and both findings are structurally unfixable inside a vendor cloud product, so the respondent reaches the conclusion without being led to it.
- **So what:** it must be live before GAIN on 15 September 2026, which is eleven days out, and that deadline sets the build priority for the entire asset library.

# SLIDE 9, Assets And Collaterals Library Dashboard, The Channels Were Replaced

- The source channel set of LinkedIn automation, cold email and LinkedIn Ads is close to the worst available mix in this market for a two person vendor selling a committee purchase to twenty named accounts, and it was replaced rather than extended.
- Automated sequencing to a Saudi CISO reads as disrespect and burns the founder's own profile, cold email has negligible response where senior executives do real business in person and on WhatsApp, and paid ads optimise for volume against a market of twenty accounts.
- Seven channels replace them, ordered by expected yield. Events and physical presence at thirty five percent, warm introduction and the sponsor network at thirty percent, founder led organic LinkedIn and X at fifteen percent, WhatsApp after introduction at ten percent, channel partner co sell at ten percent, Etimad in year two, and the owned bilingual layer underpinning all of it.
- The largest single build is the Compliance Evidence Pack, six weeks, covering NCA ECC and CCC control mapping, NDMO classification, the PDPL architecture note, the SAMA outsourcing annex, CCSPR conformity, the audit log schema and the escrow deed. It is built once and reused in every deal.
- The highest converting asset in the library is a CISO to CISO reference call that Wolffish does not attend, and it is negotiated into the design partner agreement at signature rather than requested later.
- **So what:** the full year one library is about twenty five weeks of part time founder effort, so the build sequence matters more than the list, and the first five items exist only to be ready for GAIN.

# SLIDE 10, Pricing And Packaging Dashboard, Three Lines And One Urgent Fix

- The value metric is the employee seat at SAR 79 per month, flat at every volume, with the deployment as a one time secondary metric and the token quota as a capacity selector that changes what the customer pays their own provider and not what they pay Wolffish.
- Usage based pricing is rejected on strategic grounds. The moment a token generates Wolffish revenue, the interests that make the pitch believable are inverted, and the absence of a meter is the product.
- The Decoy Hero Anchor tier model is discarded and replaced with a deployment scope ladder, Pilot at SAR 25,000 credited against conversion, Standard at SAR 60,000, and Complex at SAR 95,000 to 150,000, because the current flat fee charges the same for a clean two integration build as for a six integration multi entity deployment with a SAMA approval attached.
- Landed cost is SAR 124 to 184 per employee per month all in. Breakeven at three hundred seats requires a productivity gain of nought point seven seven percent, against Microsoft's own users reporting twenty to forty percent administrative time savings from a product that only drafts.
- One PDPL enforcement decision costs SAR 5 million. Running agents for three hundred employees costs SAR 464,400 a year landed. One avoided finding pays for roughly ten years.
- **So what:** the most urgent open action in this entire suite is the capability allowance, because ongoing capability development is currently sold as unbounded senior engineering time against a fixed recurring price, and unbounded it consumes a full principal year across only four customers.

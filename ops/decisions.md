# Decision log

What was decided, when, and why, so settled strategy is not relitigated. Newest at the top. Every
entry carries an explicit note of what it did not change, because the most common way a decision
gets reopened is somebody assuming it settled more than it did.

Seeded on 2026-09-04 from the go-to-market build. Every call below was already settled by the
source material or resolved with the founder during the build.

---

## 2026-09 Kingdom of Saudi Arabia only (DECIDED)

Every ICP, price, channel, instrument and claim in this brain is scoped to Saudi Arabia. The brain
refuses to produce for the wider Gulf, Egypt or a global audience, and says why when asked.

The rationale is not market size, it is that the entire wedge is regulatory. The argument that wins
in Riyadh, that no transfer mechanism is required because nothing crosses the border, is an
argument about specific Saudi instruments. Ported to another jurisdiction it becomes a generic
privacy claim competing against vendors with certifications this company does not hold.

**What this does not change:** nothing about the product, which is jurisdiction-neutral. A later
decision to open a second market is a channel and evidence decision, not an architecture one.

## 2026-09 The category is in-perimeter agentic workforce, not AI spend governance (DECIDED)

Positioning leads with where the work happens and what it is allowed to do. Spend control is a
supporting pillar for the finance director, never the category.

Spend governance is a budget-line purchase that a security officer does not sponsor, and the buyer
who sponsors this product is the security officer. It also collides directly with the second offer
block, which is held separately.

**What this does not change:** the quota, spend visibility and forecastable cost capabilities all
remain, and they still carry the finance director's value proposition.

---

## 2026-09 The offer matrix conflict, founder block governs (DECIDED, open for confirmation)

The Service and Offer Matrix holds two self-rated blocks describing two different companies. The
founder block, a deployed agent platform inside the customer perimeter, matches the pricing
document exactly and is treated as the sold product. The second block, an AI spend-governance
vendor, has no row appearing anywhere in the pricing document at any price, and is held as a
candidate adjacent offer rather than merged.

This is the largest repricing risk in the build. If the second block were the product, the pricing
model would be wrong by roughly an order of magnitude.

**What this does not change:** two of the second block's five rows survive in altered form inside
the platform control plane. The block is flagged, not deleted.

**Downstream:** confirm or reject the second block on the next review call. Until then no asset
describes the company as a spend-governance vendor.

## 2026-09 Twenty named accounts, not a funnel (DECIDED)

The go-to-market is built around a named account list of roughly twenty organisations rather than a
volume funnel with conversion rates.

Delivery capacity is four to six deployments in twelve months. A funnel that produces forty
qualified opportunities against a six-deployment ceiling produces thirty-four damaged
relationships in a market where the same twenty buyers meet each other. The binding constraint is
delivery, so the go-to-market is shaped to it.

**What this does not change:** the owned assets, the scorecard and the calculator still exist and
still scale, because they cost founder time once rather than per account.

---

## 2026-09 Three channels refused outright (DECIDED)

Automated outreach sequencing, volume cold email and paid social are refused, not deprioritised.
The brain declines requests to produce for them.

Sequencing aimed at a Saudi security officer reads as disrespect in a business culture built on
personal introduction, and it degrades the founder profile that the two largest channels depend
on. Cold email has negligible response where senior executives conduct real business in person and
on messaging apps. Paid social optimises for volume against a total addressable market of about
twenty accounts.

**What this does not change:** email remains in use as a confirmation and document medium after a
conversation, and messaging apps remain in the mix after an introduction has landed.

## 2026-09 Neutralise the national champion, do not compete with it (DECIDED)

No asset positions Wolffish against HUMAIN. The approved handling is one sentence on model
portability, then move on: different products, we run on the national model if that is what you
want, and you will probably buy both.

Competing on sovereignty against a state-backed national champion is a contest this company loses
on every axis that is not architecture, and losing it publicly costs the partner relationships
that ICP 2 depends on.

**What this does not change:** the architectural claim stands on its own merits against every
other competitor, and support for a sovereign in-Kingdom endpoint remains a shipped model option.

---

## 2026-09 The security call moves to stage two, before the economic conversation (DECIDED)

The security call sits at stage two of a seven-stage journey, before price is discussed in any
depth, and it is held with the security team in the room or it is rescheduled.

In Saudi regulated enterprises the security officer is the blocking role rather than a late
reviewer. A deal that reaches commercial terms before security has seen the architecture is a deal
that gets re-run from the start, and the second run is slower than the first would have been.

**What this does not change:** the finance director remains a separate audience with a separate
value proposition, reached with the landed cost breakdown after the security call.

## 2026-09 Seat pricing, published, with usage pricing refused (DECIDED)

SAR 79 per seat per month, fixed, published on the site, with inference billed to the customer at
zero Wolffish margin. Usage pricing is refused on strategic rather than economic grounds.

Forecastable cost is one of only two vectors this company leads, and its credibility rests entirely
on taking no margin on inference. The moment a token generates Wolffish revenue the argument
inverts and a finance director will spot it. Publishing the price in a market of quote-only
competitors is itself the positioning move.

**What this does not change:** movement remains available on term length, scope inclusions and
quota tier support. Saudi enterprise negotiation expects movement and refusing all of it reads as
inflexible.

---

## 2026-09 Landed cost stated on both sides, always (DECIDED)

No comparison, quotation or asset states the seat price alone. All three lines appear, with the
pass-through named as customer-billed at zero margin.

The seat price understates actual spend by roughly a third. A finance director who discovers that
after the fact discounts every other number the company has given them.

**What this does not change:** the seat price is still the headline metric in the pricing model,
because it is what tracks value and what a directory can audit.

---

## 2026-09 The adapter set is assumed, not confirmed (OPEN)

Adapters were written for Claude Code, tools reading AGENTS.md, Cursor, Windsurf, GitHub Copilot,
Gemini, and a portable control layer for chat models with no file access.

Nobody has confirmed which tools are actually in use. An adapter for a tool nobody runs goes stale
unnoticed, and a missing adapter means somebody ships copy that nothing checked.

**Downstream:** confirm the real tool list, delete the adapters nobody uses, and record the answer
here.

## Open, owned elsewhere

These are deliberately unanswered rather than invented. An open question recorded is a working
brain. An open question guessed becomes a rule, and a wrong rule propagates into every asset.

1. **The capability allowance.** The quarterly bound on ongoing capability development is proposed
   and not contracted. Owner: founder, with counsel. Until it is contracted the phrase "no new
   invoice" does not appear in writing, and four customers can consume a full principal year.
2. **The exposure and spend diagnostic delivery model.** Founder-led, self-assessment, or both.
   Owner: founder. It changes the top of the funnel and the scorecard specification.
3. **Arabic-first coverage per surface.** Interface, prompting, output and document handling are
   each flagged PARTIAL and the exact state of each is unknown. Owner: product. A Saudi buyer tests
   this in the first meeting.
4. **Escrow agent selection.** The deed is drafted and no agent is appointed. Owner: founder, with
   counsel. It is a stage-five blocker, not a stage-one one.
5. **The second offer block.** Confirmed as an adjacent offer or rejected as a category. Owner:
   founder. See the offer matrix entry above.

# Competitive market analysis

Distils the competitive dashboard. Load this for the competitor set, the vectors, and the wedge.
Per-rival talk tracks are in `competitor-battlecards.md`.

## The set

Seven options a Saudi buyer actually shortlists, which is not the same as a global category peer
group. They are six different kinds of thing, and the buyer compares them in one meeting anyway.

Microsoft's bundled assistant. OpenAI's enterprise product. Anthropic's enterprise product and
coding agent. The national artificial intelligence champion. A Saudi systems integrator building
it bespoke. Developer coding tools. A do-it-yourself open-source stack.

## The five vectors

| Vector | Wolffish | Microsoft | OpenAI | Anthropic | National champion | Integrator | Dev tools | Open source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frontier model capability | 5.2 | 8.4 | 9.1 | **9.6** | 7.3 | 5.8 | 8.9 | 6.4 |
| Ecosystem breadth | 2.6 | **9.7** | 8.2 | 6.9 | 6.1 | 7.8 | 5.4 | 3.2 |
| Sovereign legitimacy | 6.8 | 5.9 | 3.4 | 2.7 | **9.8** | 8.6 | 1.9 | 7.1 |
| Forecastable cost per employee | **9.4** | 6.7 | 4.2 | 2.1 | 4.8 | 3.6 | 3.1 | 5.9 |
| In-perimeter execution and reach | **9.6** | 3.8 | 2.9 | 4.3 | 5.2 | 7.4 | 3.7 | 6.8 |

Five vectors rather than the conventional four. Sovereign legitimacy is added because in the
Kingdom it decides deals, and a map that omits it flatters Wolffish and then loses in the room.

**Concede the first three loudly.** The concession is what makes the last two believable. A 5.2 on
model capability is correct: Wolffish runs open weights chosen for cost per token, which is a
deliberate trade. A 2.6 on ecosystem breadth is correct and will not improve.

## The two findings that govern the sales motion

**The nearest competitor on the vectors Wolffish leads is the Saudi systems integrator**, at 3.6
and 7.4, not any of the artificial intelligence vendors. Most deals are won or lost on product
economics against project economics, and the integrator is usually not in the room.

**Sovereignty is neutralised, not won.** Model portability makes the national Arabic model on an
in-Kingdom endpoint a supported configuration. That converts the hardest objection in the market
into a configuration line, and it is the single most useful sentence in the competitive file.

## The capability grid, in summary

Eighteen consolidated capabilities scored across the eight options. Two rows carry the whole
argument.

**An action-level audit log owned and stored by the customer** is matched by exactly one option,
the integrator, and only when they build it deliberately.

**Ongoing capability development without a new invoice** is matched by none. This is also the row
under the largest commercial caution in the brain, because the allowance that bounds it is not yet
contracted.

Three rows where Wolffish is behind and should say so: frontier model quality, ecosystem breadth,
and any certification. A grid that shows Wolffish ahead everywhere is a grid nobody believes.

## Shelf life

Three competitive positions have a known expiry and the positioning is deliberately not built on
them. Microsoft's Saudi cloud region is announced for the fourth quarter of 2026. One large cloud
provider has committed a regional investment and has not launched. One frontier vendor is
expanding its data-residency list.

The residency argument is therefore a two-to-four-quarter window. The no-vendor-data-path and
on-device-execution arguments are not, which is why the positioning rests on the second pair.

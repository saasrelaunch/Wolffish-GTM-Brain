# Value proposition, ICP 1

Distils the value proposition framework for the founder-led audience: Saudi-owned regulated
enterprises of 200 to 600 knowledge workers. **Never blend with ICP 2 in one asset.**

Three pillars, three reasons to believe each. Approved copy is in `../ops/copy-bank.md` and should
be used verbatim rather than rewritten, because those lines have already passed the claim ledger.

## Brand promise

Every employee gets an agent that does the work, on their own machine, and nothing of yours ever
leaves the building. A working agentic workforce inside your perimeter in six to eight weeks, on a
bill you set in advance.

## Pillar one, nothing of yours leaves the building

For the security officer. Data residency stops being a contractual promise the buyer has to defend
and becomes an architecture they can point at.

1. Agents execute on the employee's own machine and inference goes to a zero-retention endpoint on
   the customer's account. No path exists by which a prompt, a file or an output reaches Wolffish.
2. The action-level audit log lives in the customer's own store, attributed to an employee and a
   timestamp, with approval gates on sensitive operations and a kill switch per user and globally.
3. Because nothing crosses the border, no transfer mechanism is required, public-sector data stays
   conformant including for caching, and the evidence pack is shorter than any alternative.

The line that carries it: everyone offers residency, we offer the absence of a data path. Deliver
it as a diagram the security officer can trace with a finger, then stop talking.

## Pillar two, a bill you write rather than receive

For the finance director. On a metered plan the more the agents work the more the company pays, so
finance ends up rationing exactly the behaviour that creates the value.

1. SAR 79 per seat per month, fixed, with a hard token quota per person, team and period, enforced
   as a cap rather than raised as an alert.
2. Inference bills to the customer's own account at open-weight rates far below frontier models.
   SAR 30 of tokens buys roughly a hundred million input tokens with caching.
3. Landed cost is SAR 124 to 184 per employee per month, everything included.

Two disciplines. Volunteer the pass-through line before they find it. And say plainly that
Microsoft's assistant is cheaper per seat, then compare what each is allowed to do.

## Pillar three, an agent that reaches the systems that run your company

For the chief executive and the technology lead. Every vendor offers connectors: a catalogue built
for the average customer and scoped to what is safe to expose to a multi-tenant platform.

1. First-class integrations built for the customer's stack during deployment, with write access,
   their domain model and approval gates they define. Credentials stay inside the perimeter and are
   never issued to Wolffish.
2. One persistent agent per employee on their own machine with real file and command permissions
   and an Arabic-first interface, PARTIAL today across four surfaces.
3. One to two weeks scoping, three to six weeks build, then a pilot cohort before the company.

The question that opens it: which system has every integration project refused to touch?

## Matching pillar to chair

Pillar one to the security officer. Pillar two to the finance director. Pillar three to the chief
executive or technology lead. The wrong pillar in the right chair reads as not having understood
who is in the room, and it is the most common avoidable failure in this market.

## Claims not yet safe to ship

**"No new invoice"** for ongoing capability development. The allowance that bounds it is proposed
and not contracted. Use "scoped and delivered inside the quarter" until it is.

**Arabic-first described as complete.** PARTIAL across interface, prompting, output and document
handling. A Saudi buyer will test it in the first meeting, and a partial implementation described
as complete is worse than a partial one described accurately.

**Six to eight weeks** applies to a standard scope with up to three integrations into a clean
identity estate. A complex multi-entity deployment is ten to fourteen weeks. Name the scope.

# Positioning verdict, the fork that was settled

Distils the offer metrics audit. Load this when someone proposes repositioning the company, or
asks why Wolffish is not sold as a cost-control product.

## The fork

The source Service and Offer Matrix contains two self-rated blocks by two people, and they
describe two different companies.

**Reading A, the founder block.** A deployed agent platform inside the customer's perimeter. Five
rows: the security call, the deployment, the platform seat, ongoing capability development, source
access and escrow. This matches the September 2026 pricing document exactly, line for line.

**Reading B, the second block.** An artificial intelligence agent spend-governance vendor. Five
rows covering a governance platform, a spend diagnostic, guardrail design, an optimisation
retainer, and rollout support. Not one of the five appears in the pricing document at any price,
in any tier, under any name. The sheet itself flags the block as a first-pass read rather than a
live product audit.

## The verdict

**Reading A governs.** Decided 2026-09-04, recorded in `../ops/decisions.md`.

## The reasoning, which is the part that matters

The two readings put Wolffish in two different rooms.

Spend governance is the financial-operations room. The competitors are cost-observability vendors
and every monitoring platform adding an artificial intelligence cost module. The buyer is a
financial-operations lead. The ceiling is set by how much the customer is currently overspending,
which for a 300-seat Saudi enterprise is a small number.

A deployed agent platform is the security officer and chief executive room. The competitors are
Microsoft, the national champion and the systems integrator. The buyer is a committee with a
capital budget. The ceiling is set by headcount.

The second room is larger, harder to enter, and the only one in which SAR 79 per employee per
month is a rational price. Adopting the first framing would reprice the company by roughly an
order of magnitude.

Cost control is real and it is one of the two vectors Wolffish leads. It is reason four of eight
in the pricing document, not the category.

## What this does not change

The cost argument keeps its full weight. The hard enforced quota, the pass-through at zero margin,
and the landed-cost comparison stay in pillar two of the value proposition and stay in the
security-call sequence.

Two rows from Reading B survive in altered form. The spend diagnostic is promoted to a co-equal
entry offer alongside the security call, extended with the residency dimension the financial
reading missed, and becomes the Kingdom AI Exposure Scorecard. Rollout and procurement support
becomes the Compliance Evidence Pack, embedded and free rather than sold, because charging to
clear the buyer's own regulator signals that clearing it is optional.

Three rows do not survive. The governance platform is already two capabilities of the control
plane. Guardrail design is deployment work already sold inside the deployment fee. The
optimisation retainer competes for the same principal hours as an already-uncapped commitment and
is recommended against entirely.

## Still open

Whether Reading B is formally rejected as a category, or retained as a named adjacent offer, is
open for confirmation on the review call. Until then this brain executes Reading A and flags the
question wherever it bites.

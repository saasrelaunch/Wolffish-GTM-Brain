# Lead magnet, the Kingdom AI Exposure Scorecard

Distils the lead magnet specification. Load this when producing, revising or reporting on the
scorecard. Arabic name: مقياس انكشاف الذكاء الاصطناعي في المملكة

## What it is and why it converts

Twelve questions, four minutes, Arabic-first. Produces a composite exposure score out of one hundred
plus two independent sub-scores: **residency exposure** and **cost trajectory**.

Twelve rather than the conventional five to seven, because the score has two dimensions that must
each be measured credibly. A six-question composite produces a number a security officer would not
carry to an audit committee, and a number they will not carry internally has no value.

**It does not argue for Wolffish.** It measures two things, and both findings are structurally
unfixable inside a vendor-cloud product: cross-border exposure cannot be resolved by upgrading a
tier because the data path is the product, and cost trajectory cannot be resolved on a metered plan
because the meter is the business model. The respondent reaches the conclusion without being led to
it, which in a market this sceptical of vendor claims is worth more than any argument.

## The scoring design

Six residency questions, six cost questions, alternating so it does not read as two surveys stapled
together.

Three answers carry the design. **"We do not know" scores highest** on the tool inventory question,
because unknown exposure is worse than known exposure, and saying so in the scoring is the first
insight the respondent receives. **"We believe our vendor contract covers it"** is the most common
and most dangerous answer on the transfer-basis question, because a data processing agreement is not
a transfer basis. **"Classified, but artificial intelligence decisions are made separately"** is the
single most common gap in Saudi enterprise: classification exists on paper and does not reach the
decision.

## Three result tiers

| Score | Tier | Call to action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 39 | Governed | Soft. The vendor question checklist, the monthly briefing, re-score in six months. |
| 40 to 69 | Exposed | Medium. Book a security call with the security team in the room. Request the evidence pack. |
| 70 to 100 | Unmapped | Strong. The free one-week Agent Exposure and Spend Audit, founder-led, theirs to keep. |

**The strongest tier does not offer a product.** A high scorer offered a trial takes the trial and
never speaks to anyone.

Sub-score override: if either sub-score exceeds eighty while the composite lands lower, escalate to
the higher tier and name the reason. A respondent with negligible cost exposure and severe residency
exposure needs the audit, and a composite would hide that.

## The follow-up sequence

Seven steps over one hundred and eighty days. Saudi timing rules apply throughout.

The working week is Sunday to Thursday. **No sends on Friday or Saturday.** During Ramadan, sends
move to the evening where engagement is materially higher, and nothing lands during either religious
holiday.

Email delivers, messaging is where the reply happens, and the sequence is built around that
asymmetry rather than against it. **Wolffish is named as a product only once, at step four.**

**Never use a messaging app before an introduction or a volunteered number.** An unsolicited
message to a Saudi executive costs more goodwill than any email recovers.

## Implementation constraints

Arabic default for Saudi traffic, with an English toggle always visible. Not an English default with
an Arabic option. Right-to-left layout verified on real devices, and mixed Arabic and Latin numerals
checked specifically, because that is the most common rendering failure.

**Lead capture after the score is shown, never before.** Gating roughly halves completion and, more
importantly, breaks the promise that the instrument is useful in its own right.

No phone number field. Asking before a relationship exists reads as intrusive here and depresses
completion. Benchmark bands are modelled from published market data and **must be labelled as
modelled** until a respondent sample exceeds fifty.

## The dependency

This is the scaled form of the spend diagnostic promoted in the offer audit. **If the review call
rejects that promotion, this should not be built.**

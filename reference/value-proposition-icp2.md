# Value proposition, ICP 2

Distils the value proposition framework for the channel-assisted audience: regulated large
enterprises and government-adjacent entities of 600 to 2,500 knowledge workers.
**Never blend with ICP 1 in one asset.** ICP 1 buys speed and a single decision. ICP 2 buys
evidence and a defensible evaluation trail. An asset written for both reads as written for neither.

The same three pillars as ICP 1, differently weighted, plus two additions and one unanswered
blocker.

## What changes in the weighting

**Architecture arrives first and carries more of the load.** An enterprise architect can rule
Wolffish out as non-standard before any commercial discussion begins. The strongest single asset
for this audience is an architecture document showing that Wolffish adds **no new external
dependency**, which is unusual enough that it should be stated explicitly rather than assumed.

**Cost is a group-level argument, not a per-seat one.** The finance conversation is about
consolidating scattered departmental spend into one forecastable line across entities, and about a
per-employee number that survives a finance committee's challenge at 2,000 seats as well as at 300.

**Integration depth becomes a phasing argument.** Which systems in phase one, which in phase two,
and what the reachability of each does to the rollout schedule.

## Addition one, conformity for public-sector data

The cloud regulations forbid transfer of public-sector data outside the Kingdom for any purpose,
permanently or temporarily, including caching and redundancy. That single provision disqualifies
most of the competitive set outright for a government-adjacent buyer.

Wolffish does not move the data at all, so the provision is satisfied by architecture rather than
by a control. State it as a fact about the design, never as a competitive attack.

## Addition two, procurement position

A Saudi entity faces no regional-headquarters barrier and benefits from local-bidder preference.
Foreign vendors selling direct do not clear the same bar for state-entity contracts above the
threshold.

Local content documentation is prepared before it is requested. Weighting on technology services
begins in 2027 and extends further in 2028, and several large private buyers already apply their
own preferences.

This is a fact about market structure. Present it in a tender response and in a procurement
conversation, and nowhere else. It is not a customer benefit and framing it as one reads badly.

## The blocker with no answer yet

**Absence of a reference at comparable scale.** It is the hardest ICP 2 objection in year one and
nothing in the product answers it.

The only artefact that does is a security-officer-to-security-officer call from an ICP 1 design
partner. This is why the design partners matter more than their revenue suggests, and why the
design-partner agreement asks for that call at signature rather than requesting it later.

Until one exists, the honest answer is that there is no comparable reference, followed by escrow,
source access, and an offer to scope a single business unit rather than the group.

## Three roles that do not exist in ICP 1

The enterprise architect, who blocks before evaluation begins and is satisfied by the
no-new-dependency argument. Internal audit, who blocks late and expensively and is satisfied by
the audit log schema plus a walkthrough of how a specific past action would be reconstructed. The
programme management office, which is not hostile but is sequencing, and which defers you two
quarters if you arrive after the budget cycle has closed.

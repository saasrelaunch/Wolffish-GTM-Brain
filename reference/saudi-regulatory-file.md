# The Saudi regulatory file

Distils the research dossier. Load this before any compliance claim, any security call, and any
tender response. In the Kingdom this file outranks the product comparison, because it decides
which vendors are permitted before it decides which are best.

**Working reading as at September 2026, not legal advice.** Confirm anything load-bearing with
Saudi counsel before it enters a contract or a submission.

## The data rules

**The personal data law.** Personal data may leave the Kingdom only to a country assessed as
adequate, or under an approved transfer mechanism. **No adequacy list has been published.** Fines
reach SAR 5 million per breach and double on repeat, and the authority issued forty-eight
enforcement decisions in the year to early 2026.

Consequence: every prompt containing a customer name, an employee record or a patient identifier
becomes a cross-border transfer question that the buyer's data protection officer must build and
defend for a foreign vendor.

**The national data governance framework.** Four classification levels. Classification drives
storage location, encryption, retention and whether a dataset may leave the Kingdom at all.
Anything above the public level needs a documented location argument.

**The national cybersecurity controls.** Apply to government entities, their companies, and
private operators of critical national infrastructure. The security officer must evidence controls
over a third party executing code against company data.

**The cloud regulations.** Providers register in four categories against customer data levels.
Public-sector data may not be transferred outside the Kingdom **for any purpose, permanently or
temporarily, including caching or redundancy**. This is close to fatal for a foreign multi-tenant
cloud selling to government or semi-government, because caching alone breaches it.

**The central bank framework.** Prior approval is required before using cloud services **or
signing the contract**, hybrid and public, and it is not conditioned on materiality. Cloud is to be
used in-Kingdom in principle. For every bank, finance company, insurer and takaful operator, a
vendor-cloud rollout is a regulator-approval project measured in quarters.

## The derived fact everything rests on

The difference between **contractual** residency and **architectural** residency is not a
marketing nuance in this market. It is the difference between a compliance artefact the buyer has
to defend and one they can point at.

From the security officer's chair, the SAR 79 seat is partly a purchase of a shorter compliance
argument.

## The procurement rules

A foreign company without a licensed regional headquarters in the Kingdom cannot be awarded a
central-government or state-entity contract above the threshold. Foreign bidders generally need to
be materially cheaper than the nearest local competitor. Local content weighting on technology
services begins in 2027 and extends further in 2028.

Wolffish is a Saudi entity, so none of these barriers apply to it and all of them apply to several
competitors. State this as market structure in a tender or a procurement conversation, and nowhere
else. It is not a customer benefit.

Compliant electronic invoicing in riyals from a Saudi registration holder is processed as routine.
A foreign-currency invoice from a foreign entity is an exception requiring extra internal
treatment. This is a quiet, real and rarely articulated advantage.

## The demand file

Eighty-one percent of Saudi enterprises already deploy artificial intelligence in some form.
Ninety-one percent report initiatives meeting or exceeding expectations. Seventy-one percent have
participated in a pilot that delivered value, above the global average.

**The market is past the "why artificial intelligence" conversation** and into "why this one, and
will the regulator and the finance director both sign it". That is the conversation Wolffish is
built to win, and the one a capability-led plan would waste its budget restarting.

## Shelf life

Three positions expire. A major cloud region is announced for the fourth quarter of 2026. One
large provider has committed a regional investment and has not launched. One frontier vendor is
expanding its residency list.

The residency argument is a two-to-four-quarter window. The no-vendor-data-path and
on-device-execution arguments are not, which is why the positioning rests on the second pair.

# Campaigns and asset plan

Distils the assets library. Load this to place work in a channel and to know what is already
planned. Method behind the primary channel is in `relationship-engine.md`.

## The seven channels, by expected share of qualified pipeline

| Channel | Share | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Events and physical presence | ~35% | Two fixed dates carry the year |
| Warm introduction and the sponsor network | ~30% | The actual top of the funnel |
| Founder-led organic writing, bilingual | ~15% | Makes the introduction possible |
| Messaging apps, after introduction only | ~10% | An accelerant across the others |
| Channel partner co-sell | ~10% in year one, more later | Converts ICP 2 from unreachable to reachable |
| Public tender | Year two | Registration must precede a live tender, not follow it |
| Owned: site, scorecard, calculator | Underpins all | The forwardable layer |

**Refused: automated outreach sequencing, volume cold email, paid social.** Reasoning in
`relationship-engine.md`. There are no prompts for them in `../prompts/common-tasks.md`, which is a
rule expressed as an absence.

## The build sequence, by return per week of founder time

1. **The Kingdom AI Exposure Scorecard.** Two weeks. The only asset that scales without founder
   time, and it has a fixed event deadline.
2. Stand panel and speaking abstract. One week. Same deadline.
3. Landed cost calculator. Two weeks. The most forwardable asset, and it must survive reaching a
   finance director with no context.
4. Sponsor map and the forwardable paragraph. One week. Costs almost nothing and gates everything.
5. One-page bilingual leave-behind. One week.
6. Bilingual site, Arabic-first. Three weeks. Publishing the three price lines is itself a
   positioning move against a market of quote-only competitors.
7. **Compliance Evidence Pack.** Six weeks, the largest single build and the highest return. It is
   what makes the second deal's evaluation materially shorter than the first.
8. Deployment scope ladder and statement of work. Two weeks. Must exist before the next contract.
9. Pre-packaged escrow deed. Four weeks, runs in parallel with item seven.
10. Regulation explainer series. Ongoing, one post a week, starting immediately. Compounds.
11. Audit method pack. Two weeks. Needed before the third diagnostic, not the first.
12. Partner proposition one-pager. One week, and only when a specific partner is in view. Building
    enablement speculatively is a common and expensive mistake.
13. Tender registration and response template. Seven weeks. Year two, but registration must be
    complete before a relevant tender appears.

A complete year-one library is roughly twenty-five weeks of part-time founder effort, which
against a delivery ceiling of four to six deployments is the right order of magnitude. **The
sequence matters more than the list.**

## The assets that carry the most weight

**The security call.** Not an asset in the usual sense, and the pivotal one in the funnel.

**The Compliance Evidence Pack.** Built once, reused in every deal, and reviewed by Saudi counsel
before first external use.

**The security-officer-to-security-officer reference call.** The highest-converting asset in the
library, negotiated into the design-partner agreement at signature rather than requested later,
and Wolffish does not attend.

**The forwardable paragraph.** Under eighty words, no attachment, no tracked link.

**The emailed cost breakdown.** What reaches the finance director who was not in the room.

## Sequencing note

Two channels carry sixty-five percent of pipeline and neither can be automated. Plan the founder's
calendar first and the asset backlog second, not the reverse.

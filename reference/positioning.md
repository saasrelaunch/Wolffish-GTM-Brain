# Positioning

Distils the positioning dashboard. Load this for the category claim, the six statements, and the
order in which they are delivered.

## The category

**In-perimeter agentic workforce platform.** In Arabic, منصة وكلاء ذكيين داخل نطاق منشأتك

The name excludes every competitor by construction. In-perimeter excludes the four vendor clouds.
Workforce excludes the developer coding tools. Platform excludes the integrator project. Nothing
on a Saudi shortlist can adopt the label without changing what it is.

Three labels are refused. Not "AI assistant", which is the commoditised harness framing and
invites a comparison with a bundled productivity tool. Not "AI agent spend governance", which puts
the company in a different room selling to a different buyer. Not "sovereign AI", which belongs to
the national champion.

## The six statements

Each defends a different layer against a different alternative. A single line covering all four
layers would be too abstract to survive a meeting.

**1. Against the vendor clouds.** Not a smarter chat window, an employee who happens to be
software. Their products draft inside a document and answer inside a tab, and both leave people as
the copy-paste layer between an artificial intelligence and their actual job. The difference is not
how clever the model is. It is what the model is allowed to touch.

**2. Against the residency answer every vendor gives.** Not "your data is resident", your data has
nowhere to go. Every vendor offers a contractual commitment about how they will handle data after
receiving it. Wolffish never receives it, so no transfer mechanism is required. *Deliver this as an
architecture diagram the security officer can trace with a finger, then stop talking. Delivered as
a slogan it collapses.*

**3. Against the metered stack.** Not a cheaper invoice, an invoice you write instead of receive.
On a metered plan the more the agents work the more you pay, so finance rations exactly the
behaviour that creates the value. The saving is real. The forecastability is the point.

**4. Against the systems integrator.** Not a project that ends, a product that keeps arriving.
They will build it well, once. Then the second use case is a new statement of work and the fourth
is a new procurement cycle. *This decides the most deals and is the one most often skipped, because
the integrator is rarely in the room and is rarely named by the buyer as a competitor. Ask
directly: who owns this in year three, and what does the fourth use case cost?*

**5. Against the national champion.** Not instead of the national platform, the layer that puts it
on every desk. If you want the national model on an in-Kingdom endpoint, we run on it. Sovereign
infrastructure and a deployed workforce are different products. You will probably buy both.
*Never position against it. The gap on sovereign legitimacy cannot be closed, only made
irrelevant. Answer before the question is asked, in one sentence, and move on.*

**6. Against the status quo.** Not "you have no artificial intelligence", you have it and cannot
see it, cap it or audit it. Eighty-one percent of Saudi enterprises already deploy it, and the
ones who have not sanctioned it have it anyway, on personal accounts, with company data in the
prompt and no transfer basis on file.

## Message hierarchy

Never more than two levels in one meeting.

| Audience | Lead with | Statement | Never lead with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Security officer | No data path out, and compliance without a transfer mechanism | 2 | Cost. It signals the project is small. |
| Finance director | Landed cost per employee, fixed and forecastable | 3 | Model capability. It invites a comparison you lose. |
| Chief executive or owner | An agent per employee, weeks not quarters, a Saudi supplier | 1 | Architecture. Give them the outcome and the timeline. |
| Technology lead | The integrator question and capability development inside the seat | 4 | Sovereignty. They will assume you mean the national champion and stop listening. |
| Anyone asking about the national champion | We run on their model if you want it | 5 | Any comparison at all. |
| Anyone who has not started | Shadow estate exposure, and the free audit | 6 | Product. Sell the diagnostic. |

## The disqualifiers

Saying these plainly and unprompted is what makes everything else believable. In a market where
every vendor overclaims, the disqualifier is the credibility instrument.

Not the best model. Not an ecosystem. Not sovereign infrastructure. Not a large company, two
people. Not a mobile-first product. Not able to take every customer, four to six a year, and if
the fit is wrong we say so on the first call rather than in month four.
